# Target journals and pre-submission checks

Impact factors, editorial policies and fees change every year — **never quote them from memory**. Before submitting, check each journal's Guide for Authors in a browser and record the findings in `docs/private/journal_check.md`.

## Candidates (grouped by positioning; choose once the results are in)

| Positioning | Journal | Fit | Check in particular |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone oncology | Journal of Bone Oncology | bone tumours — very close fit | whether purely computational studies are accepted |
| Translational / immunology | Journal of Translational Medicine | translational medicine, accepts multi-omics | requirements for purely bioinformatic studies |
| Translational / immunology | Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy | immunotherapy | whether experimental validation is required |
| Translational / immunology | Frontiers in Immunology / Frontiers in Oncology | immune microenvironment | **several sections no longer accept unvalidated purely bioinformatic biomarker studies** — check carefully |
| Tool / methods | Computers in Biology and Medicine | computational framework plus platform | bar for methodological novelty |
| Tool / methods | Briefings in Bioinformatics | tools and resources | high bar; emphasise the generality of the atlas and AI layer |
| Fallback | BMC Cancer, Scientific Reports, Discover Oncology | broad scope | stance on purely computational work |

## Checklist for every journal
- [ ] scope covers osteosarcoma, immunology and bioinformatics
- [ ] accepts purely computational / public-data studies, and under what conditions
- [ ] AI policy: disclosure requirements and location, image restrictions
- [ ] data and code availability requirements (GitHub/Zenodo mandatory?)
- [ ] figure specifications: size, format, resolution, fonts
- [ ] limits on words, figures, tables and references
- [ ] article processing charge and open-access model
- [ ] whether TRIPOD+AI or REMARK checklists are required
- [ ] current impact factor and quartile (latest JCR; the CAS partition list if the authors' institution uses it)

## Optional strengthening
Commissioning a CRO to stain a commercial osteosarcoma tissue microarray for CXCL11/CD8/ACKR3 (IHC) would widen the range of suitable journals considerably. This is the user's decision; if done, add IHC methods and an ethics statement.
